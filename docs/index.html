Use your own map files

Run the converter in your Windows LFD2 folder, then choose its private output in the browser map viewer.

py tools\inspect_bsp_lumps.py
py tools\bsp_to_browser_mesh.py

All original content stays on your computer. No copyrighted maps or asset packs are hosted here.
